ROOTED IN WELLNESS

One pair of hands, seed to shelf.

Jeffery grows every herb from seed — and he's the only one who touches them. The soil, the transplanting, the harvest, the preparation: one grower, start to finish. The seeds are saved from the previous year's crop, so you're getting third- and fourth-generation plants, selected season after season for this soil.

Farmer-direct means you can ask the person who grew it anything about what's in the bottle. No middlemen. No mystery sourcing.
