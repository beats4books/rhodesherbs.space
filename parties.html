How long is a herbal party?

Most parties run about two hours — enough time to learn, make, and mingle. Timing is flexible for your group.

How many guests can I invite?

Parties work for intimate groups and larger gatherings alike. Tell Jeffery your headcount when you book and he'll plan accordingly.

Do guests need any experience with herbs?

None at all. Parties are built for beginners — curious, relaxed, and fun. Experienced herbalists are welcome too.

What do guests take home?

Guests make their own herbal products during the party and take them home, plus they can shop the full Rhodes Herbs line.

Where do parties happen?

Jeffery brings the experience to you. Reach out with your location and date to work out the details.
