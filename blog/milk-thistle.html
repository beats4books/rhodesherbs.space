MILK THISTLE · SILYBUM MARIANUM

Milk Thistle (Silymarin): A Review of Clinical Evidence for Liver Support

Milk thistle (Silybum marianum) and its active complex silymarin are among the most researched herbal extracts for liver-related endpoints.

A 2020 narrative review summarized clinical observations in alcoholic and non-alcoholic fatty liver contexts. Multiple meta-analyses published between 2024 and 2025, including studies on metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD/NAFLD), reported reductions in ALT and AST in some populations, along with modest improvements in certain lipid and insulin-related markers.

Key takeaways from the research

  • Silymarin shows antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity in laboratory studies.
  • Enzyme reductions appear in several randomized trials, though clinical significance and consistency vary.
  • Histological (liver biopsy) benefits are not consistently demonstrated.
  • Results depend on dose, duration, formulation, and patient population.

Safety notes

Milk thistle is generally well-tolerated at standard doses. Interactions with certain medications are possible. It is not a substitute for medical treatment of liver disease.

Is milk thistle proven to reverse liver damage?

No. Current evidence does not support claims of reversing fibrosis or curing liver disease.
