MULLEIN · VERBASCUM THAPSUS

Mullein Leaf for Respiratory Comfort: Traditional Use vs. Modern Evidence

Mullein has a long history in European herbal tradition as a demulcent and mild expectorant, particularly for dry or irritative coughs.

The European Medicines Agency recognizes certain mullein flower preparations as traditional herbal medicinal products for the relief of sore throat associated with dry cough and cold, based on long-standing use rather than modern clinical trials. Laboratory research has identified mucilage (soothing) and saponins (traditionally linked to expectorant action), along with anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activity in experimental models.

Evidence status

  • Traditional use is well documented.
  • High-quality randomized human trials specifically testing mullein leaf or tea for cough or lung function are lacking.
  • Claims that mullein "detoxes," "cleans," or "heals" the lungs are not supported by clinical evidence.

Practical note

Fine hairs on the leaves can irritate the throat if the tea is not properly strained.

Can mullein replace asthma or COPD medication?

No. It is not a treatment for asthma, COPD, or any respiratory disease.
