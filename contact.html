What ingredients do you use in your tinctures?

We use sustainably sourced, organic herbs carefully blended for maximum potency — on a vegetable glycerin base and an alcohol base, with just enough alcohol to pull the herbs.

What's in the salves?

Beeswax, coconut oil, shea butter, and olive oil — infused with the same herbs as our tinctures.

How should I store my herbal salves?

Keep salves in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight for best results.

Our products are gentle and tested to be suitable for most skin types.
